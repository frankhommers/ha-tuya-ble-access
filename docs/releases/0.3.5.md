# 0.3.5 — Pause and resume fingerprints

Adds admin-only Home Assistant actions `tuya_ble_access.pause_credential` and
`tuya_ble_access.resume_credential` for non-admin fingerprints on the K3 BLE
PRO 2 Keybox (`ba2qk177`). Supply the lock device and `credential_id` from
`list_credentials` or the Credentials sensor.

The same enrolled test fingerprint opened, was physically rejected while paused,
and opened again after restoration. No re-enrollment was needed for that cycle.
The actions preserve original validity, have no automatic resume time, and
persist unknown outcomes before Bluetooth writes. Expired access cannot be
revived. Credential services now serialize enrollment, deletion, registration,
reset reporting and pause/resume operations.

**Existing registrations:** update alone cannot recover their missing original
policy. The new actions require a non-admin fingerprint newly enrolled with
0.3.5 or later. Legacy/manual registrations are rejected rather than assigning
guessed permissions or validity. Existing access remains unchanged by updating.

Ordinary PINs, cards, admin credentials and whole-person suspension are not
supported. Physical persistence across Keybox reboot/day rollover and isolation
from another enrolled credential remain untested. Temporary PIN pause/resume
from 0.3.4 is unchanged.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. Validation includes automated
policy, identity, persistence, failure and concurrency tests, package checks and
secret scans.
