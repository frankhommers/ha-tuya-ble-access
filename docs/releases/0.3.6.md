# 0.3.6 — Pause and resume cards

Extends `tuya_ble_access.pause_credential` and `tuya_ble_access.resume_credential`
to non-admin cards on the verified K3 BLE PRO 2 Keybox (`ba2qk177`). The same
actions continue to support fingerprints.

Physical validation confirmed the same card opened before pausing, was rejected
while paused, and opened after its original validity was restored. The test
fingerprint still opened while the card was paused. No re-enrollment was needed
for this cycle. Pausing preserves the original expiry and has no automatic resume.

The actions require original policy metadata captured when enrolling through
0.3.5 or later. Updating does not recover this metadata for older or manually
registered credentials. Both physical test items were restored, but their legacy
HA registrations cannot use these actions without a new supported enrollment.

Ordinary PINs, admin/limited-use access and whole-person suspension remain
unsupported. Reboot/day-rollover persistence and other isolation combinations
remain untested. No additional physical testing was performed after the Keybox
was moved indoors.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. Validation includes automated
card/fingerprint round trips, slot/type isolation, unsupported-PIN rejection,
package checks and secret scans.
